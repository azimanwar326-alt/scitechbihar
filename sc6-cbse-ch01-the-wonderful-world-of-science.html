📘 Chapter 01: The Wonderful World of Science – Simple Questions and Answers

Question 1. What is science?

Answer: Science is a systematic way of learning about the world around us. It helps us understand natural things and events through observation, questioning and experiments.


Question 2. Why is science important in our daily life?

Answer: Science is important because it makes our life easier, safer and more comfortable. It helps us in cooking, travelling, communication, farming, treatment of diseases and many other activities.


Question 3. What do scientists do?

Answer: Scientists observe things, ask questions, perform experiments and collect evidence to understand how the world works.


Question 4. How do we learn science?

Answer: We learn science by observing things around us, asking questions, doing experiments, collecting information and discussing our findings.


Question 5. What is observation?

Answer: Observation means carefully looking at something and noticing its details. We can observe things using our sense organs and different instruments.


Question 6. Why should we ask questions in science?

Answer: Asking questions helps us to develop curiosity and find reasons behind different events. Questions encourage us to investigate and learn new things.


Question 7. What is an experiment?

Answer: An experiment is a test or activity performed to find out whether an idea or explanation is correct. Experiments help us collect evidence.


Question 8. What is scientific thinking?

Answer: Scientific thinking means using observation, reasoning, evidence and experiments to understand something. It also means being ready to change our ideas when new evidence is found.


Question 9. What is curiosity?

Answer: Curiosity is the desire to know or learn something. It encourages us to ask questions and explore the world around us.


Question 10. How is science present in our surroundings?

Answer: Science is present everywhere around us. We can see it in plants, animals, water, air, sunlight, machines, vehicles, mobile phones and household activities.


Question 11. Give some examples of science in everyday life.

Answer: Some examples of science in everyday life are:

  1. Using a refrigerator to keep food fresh.
  2. Using a mobile phone to communicate.
  3. Using electricity to operate appliances.
  4. Using vehicles for transportation.
  5. Using medicines to treat diseases.
  6. Using a pressure cooker to cook food.

Question 12. How does science help farmers?

Answer: Science helps farmers through improved seeds, irrigation methods, fertilizers, farming machines, weather information and methods of protecting crops from pests and diseases.


Question 13. How does science help doctors?

Answer: Science helps doctors diagnose and treat diseases. Instruments such as thermometers, X-ray machines and other medical devices help doctors examine patients.


Question 14. How does science help us understand nature?

Answer: Science helps us understand natural events such as rainfall, the movement of the Sun and Moon, changes in weather, growth of plants and the behaviour of animals.


Question 15. Why should we not accept every claim without evidence?

Answer: We should not accept every claim without evidence because some claims may be incorrect. Science teaches us to ask questions, check facts and use evidence before reaching a conclusion.


Question 16. What is the role of experiments in science?

Answer: Experiments help us test our ideas and explanations. They provide evidence and help us understand whether our observations and conclusions are correct.


Question 17. Can science change with new discoveries?

Answer: Yes, scientific knowledge can change when new observations, experiments and evidence provide a better explanation. Science is always developing through new discoveries.


Question 18. What is the difference between a scientific fact and a guess?

Answer: A scientific fact is supported by observations and reliable evidence. A guess is an idea that has not yet been tested or supported by sufficient evidence.


Question 19. Why is teamwork important in science?

Answer: Teamwork is important because scientists can share ideas, discuss observations, perform experiments together and solve problems more effectively.


Question 20. How can students develop a scientific attitude?

Answer: Students can develop a scientific attitude by asking questions, observing carefully, performing simple experiments, respecting evidence and being open to new ideas.


Science in Everyday Life


Question 21. How does science make communication easier?

Answer: Science has developed mobile phones, computers, the internet and satellites. These technologies help people communicate quickly over long distances.


Question 22. How does science help in transportation?

Answer: Science and technology have developed bicycles, cars, trains, ships and aeroplanes. These means of transport help people and goods move from one place to another.


Question 23. How does science help in cooking food?

Answer: Science helps us understand heating, boiling, freezing and other processes used in cooking and preserving food. Appliances such as gas stoves, induction cooktops and refrigerators are also based on scientific principles.


Question 24. How does science help us during bad weather?

Answer: Scientific instruments and satellites help us observe weather conditions and prepare forecasts. Weather forecasts can warn us about storms, heavy rainfall and other natural events.


Question 25. Why is science important for the future?

Answer: Science is important for the future because it helps us solve problems related to health, food, energy, environment, transportation and communication. It also helps us develop new technologies.


Important Differences

Question 26. Differentiate between observation and experiment.

Answer: The difference between observation and experiment is given below:

Observation Experiment
It means carefully noticing something. It means performing a test to find an answer.
It may be done without changing anything. It is performed under planned conditions.
Example: Observing a plant growing. Example: Testing how sunlight affects plant growth.

Question 27. Differentiate between science and technology.

Answer: The difference between science and technology is given below:

Science Technology
Science helps us understand the natural world. Technology uses knowledge to make useful tools and devices.
It is based on observation and experiments. It is based on applying scientific and other knowledge.
Example: Understanding electricity. Example: Making an electric fan.

Application-Based Questions


Question 28. You see a plant growing towards a window. What question can you ask about it?

Answer: We can ask why the plant is growing towards the window. We can investigate whether sunlight affects the direction of plant growth.


Question 29. If you want to know whether plants need sunlight, how will you investigate?

Answer: We can keep two similar plants under different conditions. One plant should receive sunlight and the other should be kept without sunlight. We should give both plants the same amount of water and observe their growth for several days.


Question 30. A glass of water becomes cold when kept in a refrigerator. Which branch of science helps us understand this?

Answer: Physics helps us understand heat and temperature and how cooling takes place.


Question 31. Why should we repeat an experiment?

Answer: Repeating an experiment helps us check whether the results are consistent and reliable. It also reduces the chance of errors.


Question 32. What should we do if the result of an experiment is different from our expectation?

Answer: We should carefully observe the result, check the steps of the experiment and repeat it if necessary. We should accept the evidence instead of forcing the result to match our expectation.


Higher Order Thinking Questions


Question 33. Is science limited to laboratories? Give reasons.

Answer: No, science is not limited to laboratories. We can learn science from our surroundings, homes, schools, gardens, farms and nature. Everyday activities also help us understand scientific ideas.


Question 34. Why is asking questions considered the beginning of scientific learning?

Answer: Asking questions makes us curious about the things around us. It encourages us to observe, investigate and find explanations. Therefore, asking questions is an important beginning of scientific learning.


Question 35. How can science help us protect the environment?

Answer: Science helps us understand pollution, climate change and the importance of natural resources. It also helps us develop renewable energy, waste management and water conservation methods.


Question 36. Why should scientific conclusions be based on evidence?

Answer: Evidence makes scientific conclusions more reliable. It helps us distinguish between correct explanations and unsupported beliefs.


Question 37. How can a simple activity at home become a science investigation?

Answer: We can choose a question, make an observation, form an idea, perform a simple and safe activity, record the results and draw a conclusion. For example, we can observe how sugar dissolves in water at different temperatures.

Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

  1. Understand the world around us
  2. Ignore observations
  3. Avoid asking questions
  4. Believe everything without checking

  2. Guessing without checking
  3. Ignoring changes
  4. Copying an answer

Answer: A. Carefully watching a plant grow


Question 40. Which activity helps us test an idea?

  1. Experiment
  2. Guessing
  3. Ignoring
  4. Memorising

Answer: A. Experiment


Question 41. Which quality encourages us to explore new things?

  1. Curiosity
  2. Carelessness
  3. Fear
  4. Laziness

Question 42. Which of the following is based on scientific knowledge?

  1. Refrigerator
  2. Mobile phone
  3. Electric fan
  4. All of these

Answer: D. All of these


Question 43. What should we use to support a scientific conclusion?

  1. Evidence
  2. Rumours
  3. Guesswork only
  4. Imagination only

Answer: A. Evidence


Question 44. Which of the following is a scientific activity?

  1. Asking questions
  2. Observing carefully
  3. Performing experiments
  4. All of these

Answer: D. All of these

Word Simple Meaning
Science A systematic way of understanding the world.
Observation Carefully noticing something.

Chapter Summary

  2. Science is based on observation, questioning and experiments.
  3. Curiosity encourages us to explore and learn new things.
  4. Scientists use evidence to test their ideas.
  5. Science is present in our everyday life.
  6. Science helps in agriculture, medicine, communication and transportation.
  7. Scientific knowledge develops through new observations and discoveries.
  8. We should develop a scientific attitude and think logically.

Message of the Chapter

Science is not just a subject in our textbooks. It is a way of understanding the world around us. We should remain curious, ask questions, observe carefully and use evidence to find answers. Every child can learn science by exploring everyday things.
